Tool AI

Tool AI turns plain-English prompts into real working web tools. It is built for people who are not techy: describe the tool you need, and Tool AI generates a working version you can use. Examples include a student homework tracker that updates when assignments are posted, a dollar-to-Bitcoin calculator, or a grocery tracking tool. The current public link may sometimes be down while hosting and beta testing are being worked out.

About the work

I focus on making software feel direct: prompts become tools, ideas become interfaces, and prototypes become usable faster.

How I build

My style is fast and product-focused. I use AI-assisted coding, Node.js, and frontend iteration to turn vague ideas into working web experiences that people can test quickly.

The goal is not just to make demos. The goal is to build tools with clear workflows, practical defaults, and interfaces that do not require users to understand the technical parts underneath.
